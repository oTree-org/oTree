oTree can do it

oTree is a versatile framework for behavioral research that lets you build all kinds of games and tasks. Our starter library features a continuous-time double auction market, simulations of social networking sites and online shopping apps, multi-player recreational games like poker and live word search. You can easily program classic game theory games like the prisoner’s dilemma, surveys and psychological tests.

oTree has been used in over 3000 academic publications.
